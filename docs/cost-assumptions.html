MODEL REFERENCE

Cost assumptions

All rates are illustrative author assumptions, uncalibrated to a manufacturer. Total modeled cost is not delay cost, accounting profit, revenue, or contractual damages.

Default rates

CategoryRateRecorded basis
Scheduled labor$60 / person-hourNamed shift staffing × working hours, excluding breaks.
Overtime premium$30 / person-hour above baseOnly shifts explicitly designated overtime in the scenario. Default shifts are regular, so the default premium charge is zero.
Consumed materials$1,000 / aggregate kit; $10,000 / individual engineUnique material-issued posting quantities, by SKU. Every kit has its own explicit rate.
WIP carrying$100 / aircraft-dayActual admission until delivery or observed cutoff.
Accrued lateness$500 / aircraft-dayDue date until delivery or observed cutoff, including overdue released orders waiting upstream.

How to audit a charge

In Production results → Costs, click a category amount, then a charge amount. Its source view shows the exact accrued interval, quantity, applied rate version, source event IDs or calendar/scenario basis. Aircraft links open the same serial at the displayed scene time. The report date range and history cutoff limit all charges.

Two people × two scheduled hours × $60 = $240 paid labor, including idle time. Two engines × $10,000 = $20,000 at issue. Twelve elapsed hours late × $500 / 24 hours = $250.

No duplicate costs

Assigned and idle labor partition paid scheduled labor. They are not extra charges. Rework uses the same finite capacity; it does not automatically create another paid-labor charge. Base labor already includes paid overtime shift hours; only the premium is added separately.

Receipts, reservations, installation completion and replay do not charge material again. A separate replacement issue would be additional consumption. WIP carrying excludes upstream orders and raw stock. Unfinished carrying and lateness are included exposure, not extra totals or estimates of an unknown finish.

Time and rounding

Each order defaults to release + 35 plant-local calendar days, retaining local wall time across daylight saving, or uses its explicit dueAt. Charge days are 24 elapsed hours: a 23-hour local day contributes 23/24 of a daily charge. Nights and weekends count for carrying and lateness; scheduled labor follows working intervals.

Integer USD cents use exact millisecond proration and half-up rounding at stable postings. Continuous charges use differences between rounded cumulative endpoints, so adjacent date ranges reconcile. Display quantities may be abbreviated; source views show exact elapsed time. Opening a report repeatedly does not add costs.

Editing assumptions

Simulation settings → Production & costs edits hourly, daily and individual SKU rates. Apply & restart freezes those rates into the new scenario. Authored scenarios can also set costRates.overtimeShiftLabels and each order's dueAt. No weekly payroll threshold is inferred.

Limits

Equipment depreciation, taxes, procurement cash flows, raw-stock carrying, revenue and contractual damage calculations are excluded. Plant-wide paid capacity is not arbitrarily assigned to individual aircraft. Lower consumption may mean fewer deliveries, so read costs alongside deliveries and unfinished WIP. Full modeling notes are maintained in docs/cost-assumptions.md in the project repository.
