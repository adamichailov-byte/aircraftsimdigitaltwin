# Factory workcell details

The 3D scene is a visual companion to the simulation. It shows representative workcell equipment and current work activity; it is not a detailed engineering layout.

- Select a production stage or aircraft to inspect its tasks, alerts, crew, equipment, and material status. The scene has no station information boards.
- Worker figures represent assigned crew counts at active stations. The scene displays at most ten representative figures per cell; the inspector reports the modeled count. No individual people are tracked.
- Bay racks show illustrative bins while a station's issued material job is active. Store racks reflect aggregate available stock relative to opening inventory. These visuals are not physical capacity measurements or item-level delivery tracking.
- Crane, hoist, and paint-head motion follows the related task's progress. Equipment poses are illustrative and do not represent a certified robot path or machine simulation.
- Small tools and utility details may be hidden at distance or when Reduced quality is enabled.

All geometry, placement, and activity are synthetic. The scene is not a factory survey, transport clearance study, or safety assessment.
