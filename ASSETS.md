# Asset record

AD-00 uses original procedural geometry in `src/app/App.tsx`: hangar slab, walls, roof strip, columns, structural beams, floor markings and scale ruler. No external meshes, textures, fonts or imagery are downloaded or redistributed. System fonts are provided by the viewer's operating system. Three.js, Fiber and Drei dependencies retain their package license files in the dependency installation.

AD-01 moves the building geometry into `src/scene/Building.tsx` and adds original campus geometry in `src/scene/Campus.tsx`, with dimensions in `src/demo/campus.ts`. Roads, sidewalks, rail sleepers, markings, bay outlines and apron stands are original procedural meshes. Floor-label textures are created locally from text using the system font; texture resources are disposed on removal. No third-party assets were introduced.

AD-02: the fictional A-100 aircraft is original procedural geometry authored for this project. Sources: `src/models/aircraft-parts.ts` (fuselage profile, swept-wing/tail lofts, nacelle and fan shapes), `Aircraft.tsx` (named assembly groups, windows, landing gear, supports and manufacturing variants), and `materials.ts` (PBR finishes). All meshes are generated locally; no manufacturer's CAD, proprietary assets, downloaded aircraft model, image billboard or remote texture is used. Geometry source remains editable. Nominal complete-aircraft envelope: 60 m length, 60 m span, 17 m height. Separated sections extend to 64 m total length with two 2 m joint gaps; they are a display state, not a second aircraft type.

The initial cockpit panes were revised after owner critique. Forward glazing now curves across both sides of the nose in two panes, with two separate side panes. Dimensions and styling are still illustrative; no aircraft CAD is claimed.

AD-05 adds four static, fictional people made from original procedural geometry in `src/scene/Selectable.tsx`; their role and task text is illustrative, not employee or location tracking. Selection proxies, equipment/rack fixtures and highlighting are original geometry in the scene. No external assets were added.

AD-06 adds original procedural station tooling in `src/models/Tooling.tsx`: fuselage alignment rollers, a supported bridge crane and wing jig, cable reels/runs and electronics bench, an engine handling cradle and gear stands, test equipment and paint-bay filtration/markings. The existing cabin cutaway in `src/models/Aircraft.tsx` now has more seat, rail, and overhead-bin detail. Repeated box parts share instanced geometry in `src/models/RepeatedBlocks.tsx`. All dimensions and equipment are illustrative; no CAD, external mesh, texture, or machinery specification was used.

AD-07 adds original procedural transport geometry in `src/models/TransportKit.tsx`: a distinct cargo freighter, a rail consist with section and wing loads, a road truck, forklift, belt loader, material tug, aircraft tow tractor and ground-power cart. `src/models/LogisticsKit.tsx` adds local receiving cradles, route markings, dwell rings and marked pedestrian crossings; `src/demo/routes.ts` holds static centre-lines and clearance widths for later animation. The two parked delivery aircraft reuse the original A-100 model. All geometry and route data are original and illustrative; no external models, textures, logos or manufacturer assets were introduced. No licensed third-party assets were added.
