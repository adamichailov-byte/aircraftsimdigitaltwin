# Asset and model notes

The aircraft, buildings, people, equipment, vehicles, factory fixtures, and markings are generated from editable geometry in this project. They are fictional and illustrative. No manufacturer CAD, proprietary factory data, external 3D model, logo, or texture is included.

The aircraft is the fictional A-100. Its dimensions, construction details, materials, and assembly sequence are not a representation of a certified aircraft or a manufacturer's released design.

The application uses Three.js, React Three Fiber, and Drei. Their licenses are distributed with their respective packages and remain applicable to those dependencies. System fonts are supplied by the user's operating system.
